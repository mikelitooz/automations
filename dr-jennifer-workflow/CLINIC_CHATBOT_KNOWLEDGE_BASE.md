## Common Medical Questions

### When Should I Seek Medical Care?

**Schedule a Regular Appointment For:**
- Routine physical exams
- Chronic condition management
- Medication refills
- Preventive screenings
- Non-urgent health concerns

**Schedule a Same-Day/Urgent Visit For:**
- Fever over 101°F lasting more than 2 days
- Severe sore throat or ear pain
- Persistent cough or difficulty breathing
- Urinary tract infection symptoms
- Minor injuries (sprains, strains, minor cuts)
- Skin infections or rashes
- Stomach pain or persistent vomiting/diarrhea

**Go to Urgent Care or ER For:**
- Chest pain or pressure
- Difficulty breathing or shortness of breath
- Severe abdominal pain
- Head injury with loss of consciousness
- Severe allergic reactions
- High fever with stiff neck
- Severe bleeding
- Signs of stroke (facial drooping, arm weakness, speech difficulty)
- Suicidal thoughts or severe mental health crisis

**Call 911 Immediately For:**
- Chest pain that may be a heart attack
- Stroke symptoms
- Severe difficulty breathing
- Unconsciousness
- Severe bleeding that won't stop
- Major trauma or injuries

### Common Conditions We Treat

**Cold & Flu:**
- Symptoms typically last 7-10 days
- Treatment: Rest, fluids, over-the-counter medications
- Schedule appointment if: Fever over 101°F for more than 3 days, difficulty breathing, severe symptoms

**Strep Throat:**
- Symptoms: Severe sore throat, fever, swollen lymph nodes
- Diagnosis: Rapid strep test in office
- Treatment: Antibiotics if positive

**Urinary Tract Infection (UTI):**
- Symptoms: Painful urination, frequent urination, lower abdominal pain
- Diagnosis: Urine test
- Treatment: Antibiotics

**High Blood Pressure:**
- Regular monitoring required
- Lifestyle modifications
- Medication management
- Goal: Blood pressure below 130/80

**Diabetes Management:**
- Type 2 diabetes care
- A1C monitoring (goal: below 7%)
- Medication management
- Lifestyle counseling
- Complication screening

**Skin Issues:**
- Rashes, eczema, psoriasis
- Acne treatment
- Skin tag/mole removal
- Suspicious lesion evaluation

## Prescription Refills

### How to Request a Refill

**Option 1: Patient Portal (Fastest)**
1. Log in to patient portal
2. Click "Medications"
3. Select medication needing refill
4. Click "Request Refill"
5. Submit request
6. **Processing time:** 1-2 business days

**Option 2: Phone Request**
- Call: (555) 123-4567 (Press 2)
- Have ready: Medication name, dosage, pharmacy information
- **Processing time:** 2-3 business days

**Option 3: Pharmacy Direct**
- Ask your pharmacy to send a refill request to us
- **Processing time:** 2-3 business days

### Refill Policy

**Allowed Refills:**
- Most maintenance medications (blood pressure, diabetes, cholesterol meds)
- Chronic condition medications
- Thyroid medications
- Antidepressants and anxiety medications

**Require Office Visit:**
- Controlled substances (pain medications, stimulants, benzodiazepines)
- New medications (never prescribed before)
- Medications requiring monitoring (certain antibiotics, etc.)
- If you haven't been seen in over 12 months

**Refill Timing:**
- Request refills at least 3-5 days before you run out
- We cannot expedite refills requested at the last minute
- Plan ahead for weekends and holidays

**Refill Denials:**
If your refill is denied, you will be notified with the reason:
- May need an office visit
- May need updated lab work
- Medication may no longer be appropriate
- Insurance issues

**Emergency Refills:**
If you're completely out of a critical medication and it's after hours:
- Call our after-hours nurse line: (555) 123-4599
- They may authorize a small emergency supply
- Follow up with our office the next business day

- We use the California prescription monitoring program (CURES)

- Dr. Jennifer will provide a referral

- Specialist reports are sent back to our office
- Continue seeing us for primary care needs
